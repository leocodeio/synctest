# References

This file aggregates useful references for the beat‑synced video pipeline.

## Audio / Beat Detection
- **librosa.beat.beat_track** – Beat tracking and tempo estimation.
  https://librosa.org/doc/main/generated/librosa.beat.beat_track.html
- **librosa Documentation** – General usage and installation.
  https://librosa.org/doc/main/
- **Madmom** – Alternative beat tracking library (music information retrieval).
  https://madmom.readthedocs.io/

## Video Processing
- **MoviePy Documentation** – Video editing and composition.
  https://moviepy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/
- **FFmpeg** – Backend for video encoding/decoding used by MoviePy.
  https://ffmpeg.org/
- **PySceneDetect** – Content-aware scene detection and video segmentation.
  https://scenedetect.com/
- **OpenCV** – Computer vision library for motion analysis and optical flow.
  https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/
- **OpenCV Optical Flow Tutorial** – Motion intensity calculation.
  https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d4/dee/tutorial_optical_flow.html

## General Resources
- **Python Packaging** – Installing dependencies via pip.
  https://packaging.python.org/tutorials/installing-packages/
- **NumPy** – Numerical computing library used by librosa.
  https://numpy.org/
